Airspy HF+ firmware

    

      

        
The Airspy has its own firmware, separate from the ESP. Its version is shown under Receiver.

        
The Airspy HF+ Discovery exists in two variants (BB and CD). Use the file that matches your receiver. This receiver requires: —.

        
Choose the correct file (hfplus-firmware-cd.bin or hfplus-firmware-bb.bin) and click update. Verification and update reject a wrong file. The device backs up the calibration, starts the bootloader, writes and verifies the firmware, then restores the calibration. Keep the receiver connected.

0.1.0
